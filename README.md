# Earthistory 🏛️

An open-source, AI-powered historical deep web search engine built with TypeScript. **Earthistory** bridges the gap between modern technology and hidden historical archives, making ancient scripts, royal records, and deep-web historical databases accessible and instantly translatable for everyone.

This project is officially targeted to be hosted at `earthistory.js.org`.

---

## 🚀 Vision & Purpose

While general search engines excel at surface web indexing (SEO, news, and commerce), they fail to penetrate specialized, restricted, or academic historical archives (The Deep Web). **Earthistory** is designed specifically to index academic databases, institutional APIs, and digital national archives safely and efficiently.

### Key Features:
* **Deep Web Archival Indexing:** Penetrates beyond the surface web to discover raw historical manuscripts and data.
* **AI Historical Translation:** Translates ancient scripts, Pali, Latin, and old languages into clean, modern English with academic precision.
* **Ad-Free Academic Focus:** Designed purely for scholars, researchers, and students.

## 💵 Business & Social Impact Model

Earthistory operates on a unique **Targeted Monetization Strategy (Freemium B2B)** designed to empower public education while remaining economically sustainable.

### 🟢 Free Segment ($0 / Forever)
* **Public K-12 Schools & Government Education:** Complete access to all tools to foster historical interest in younger generations.
* **Private Tuitions & Independent Tutors:** Full premium access provided completely free of charge to support grassroots educators and flexible learning environments.
* **Non-Profit Learning Centers:** Free access for community-driven educational programs.

### 🏛️ Paid Segment (Institutional Premium)
* **Private & International Schools:** Custom annual subscription fees tailored to their operational budgets.
* **Universities, Colleges & Research Institutions:** Full enterprise-grade API access, high-resolution manuscript downloads, and institutional licensing on a yearly subscription basis.
